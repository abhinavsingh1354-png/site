Our Story

Soul & Bliss, in every piece

Rayka & Co began as a quiet hobby — beads, thread, a candle poured on a slow Sunday. It grew into a small studio built around one idea: handmade things carry something machines can't copy.

Every order is made or finished after you place it, so colours, charms and little details can be customized just for you.

our story

Started on a kitchen table, still made that way.

Rayka N.Co began with candles poured in small jars and jewelry threaded one bead at a time. Every wedding order, every gift box, still gets the same attention it did on day one — nothing leaves our hands until it feels right.

We keep batches small on purpose. It means slower drops, but it also means what you get was actually made for you.

— Team Rayka
